Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DailyDoseofGTA6 (“we”, “us”) handles information when our internal clip publishing tool (“the App”) connects to TikTok.

1. Who this applies to

The App is used by authorized operators of our brand accounts. It is not a public consumer app and is not intended to collect data from the general public.

2. TikTok data we use

When a TikTok account is connected through Login Kit and the Content Posting API, we may access:

  • Basic profile information (such as open_id / display name / avatar metadata provided by TikTok’s user.info.basic scope) to confirm we connected the correct brand account.
  • Upload and publish capability (TikTok Content Posting API scopes such as video.upload and video.publish, including Direct Post) so we can upload edited vertical gameplay clips and publish them to our own TikTok account only.

We do not request access to other users’ TikTok accounts, follower lists beyond what is required for posting confirmation, or private messages.

3. How we use the data

  • Authenticate and keep a connection to our brand TikTok account.
  • Upload and publish videos that we created to that account.
  • Debug failed uploads and maintain operational logs needed to run the pipeline.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not share TikTok profile data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use TikTok data to build unrelated marketing profiles.

5. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and basic account identifiers are stored securely on systems we control, with access limited to operators who run the publishing pipeline. We retain this information only while the TikTok connection is needed for publishing, and delete or revoke it when the connection is removed or upon a valid deletion request.

6. Data deletion requests

To request deletion of TikTok-related data we hold, email dailydoseofgta88@gmail.com with the subject “TikTok data deletion”. We will revoke tokens and delete associated profile identifiers from our systems within a reasonable time, subject to any legal retention requirements. You can also revoke the App’s access in your TikTok account settings.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: dailydoseofgta88@gmail.com
