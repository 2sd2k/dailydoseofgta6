Daily Dose of GTA6

DailyDoseofGTA6 is an internal publishing tool for our own social accounts. It finds notable GTA 6 gameplay moments from Twitch streams, edits them into short vertical videos, and posts them to our brand channels on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram.

What this TikTok app does

  • The brand owner signs in with our own TikTok account (@dailydoseofgtatv) using Login Kit.
  • Our pipeline uploads edited highlight clips and publishes them to that same account via the Content Posting API (Direct Post).
  • Captions credit the original Twitch streamer.
  • We do not post on behalf of other creators or end users.
